South Island Itineraries from Queenstown

Travel Queenstown to Christchurch via Lake Tekapo in about 4 days, follow the West Coast via Wānaka, Haast, glacier country, Hokitika and Arthur’s Pass in about 7 days, or take a southern east coast loop through Invercargill, The Catlins and Dunedin in about 3 days.

Private Multi-Day Tours with a Local Driver-Guide

Plan a private South Island tour around your available time, interests and preferred pace. Routes can include Queenstown, Wānaka, Milford Sound, Te Anau, Franz Josef, Fox Glacier, Hokitika, Christchurch, Akaroa, Nelson, Picton, Dunedin and Bluff.
